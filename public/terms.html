You agree to provide accurate, complete, and truthful information during registration, including your legal name, blood group, genotype, severe allergies, and Next-of-Kin (ICE) contact details. MyVault bears no liability for medical treatment decisions resulting from deliberately inaccurate information submitted by the user.

4. Emergency Authorization & Data Access

By carrying a MyVault physical credential or placing a QR sticker on your vehicle/helmet, you explicitly authorize first responders, law enforcement officers, and healthcare professionals to scan the QR code in emergency circumstances to access your vital health details and notify your designated emergency contacts.
